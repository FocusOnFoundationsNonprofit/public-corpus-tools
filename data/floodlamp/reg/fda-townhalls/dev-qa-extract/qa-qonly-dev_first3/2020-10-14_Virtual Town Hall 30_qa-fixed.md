QA Block 21
CLARIFIED QUESTION: What is the FDA's position on the off-label use of antigen testing for asymptomatic screening, including in nursing homes?
CLARIFIED ANSWER: The FDA has mixed data on the use of antigen testing for asymptomatic screening, and decisions depend on submitted data from developers. FDA has outlined pathways for authorization which include comparison studies, banked samples, and historical data showing comparable cycle thresholds. Clinicians can use these tests off-label as long as ordered under proper guidance, with policies from CLIA and CMS allowing such use, including reimbursement.
