CLARIFIED QUESTION: What updates did the FDA provide on pooling for SARS-CoV-2 tests?
CLARIFIED ANSWER: The FDA acknowledges that pooling increases testing throughput but can result in missed low positives. To ensure effectiveness, the FDA updated its templates requiring a positive percent agreement (PPA) with single tests of 85%. Developers and labs must validate and ensure balanced distributions of low and high positives matching real lab conditions. Validation data should be submitted within 15 business days after notifying the FDA that pooling has been implemented. Performance variability across labs highlights the importance of factors like the assay's sensitivity (LOD) and historical low positive rate distribution for optimizing pooling.
