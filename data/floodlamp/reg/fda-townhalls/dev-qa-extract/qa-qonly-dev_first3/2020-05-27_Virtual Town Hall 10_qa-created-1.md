CLARIFIED ANSWER: The FDA periodically reviews the notification list for COVID-19 serology tests. Tests may be removed if their manufacturers fail to submit an EUA request within a reasonable time or encounter significant problems that are not resolved promptly. Some manufacturers also voluntarily withdraw their tests, which is marked with an asterisk on the FDA's lists. The FDA actively maintains and updates these lists to reflect current distributions.

Commercial or manufacturers listed below this question did provide notification to the FDA that they had validated and intended to distribute their serology tests for infection as set forth in section 4.D of our policy. The FDA had previously included them on the Web site notification list of commercial manufacturers distributing through all of these test kids under their pharmacy, but they have now been removed from the notification list and placed on this list. QA Block 25
CLARIFIED QUESTION: What are the potential strategies for COVID-19 testing in asymptomatic individuals, including the use of pooling?
CLARIFIED ANSWER: The FDA supports the use of EUA-authorized tests ordered by healthcare providers, which can be used broadly, including for institutions such as schools and workplaces.
