Using authorized catalog numbers for primers and probes simplifies validation for both paths.

QA Block 8:
CLARIFIED QUESTION: What are the FDA recommendations for retrospective studies on rapid antigen tests regarding low positives and frozen swabs?
CLARIFIED ANSWER: The FDA template recommends a minimum of 20% low positives for retrospective studies on rapid antigen tests. Additionally, a minimum of five fresh positive samples is recommended for such studies. However, the FDA highlights challenges with using frozen swabs in prospective studies as most point-of-care sites do not have the necessary freezers, making it impractical. The FDA also noted potential evidence that freeze-thaw cycles might make more target available for certain assays, potentially improving performance.
