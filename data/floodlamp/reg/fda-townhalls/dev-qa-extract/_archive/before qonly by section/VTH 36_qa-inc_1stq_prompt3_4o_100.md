QUESTION: What guidance can the FDA provide on establishing a primer set for a LAMP-based assay and leveraging it for test validation?
ANSWER: The FDA recognizes two main pathways for this approach. Developers can seek their own authorization for the test and provide a right of reference to others, allowing them to access the data in the original submission. However, those using the test must still meet authorization requirements. Alternatively, developers can use a model like Yale's saliva direct, where the method is authorized, and specific labs designated by the sponsor can use it under the EUA. Such designated labs must adhere to commitments made to the FDA. This pathway allows flexibility while ensuring compliance with FDA requirements.
