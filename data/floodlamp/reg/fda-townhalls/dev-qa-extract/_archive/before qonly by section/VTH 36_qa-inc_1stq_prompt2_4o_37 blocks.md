CLARIFIED QUESTION: What updates does the FDA have regarding COVID-19 diagnostic test authorizations and priorities?
CLARIFIED ANSWER: The FDA has recently authorized more pooling schemes, home collection kits including one for SARS-CoV-2 and flu detection, rapid antigen tests, and serology tests. These authorizations support efficient resource use and testing in low-prevalence populations. The FDA's development and review priorities remain point-of-care tests, home collection options, panel testing, and high-throughput systems.
