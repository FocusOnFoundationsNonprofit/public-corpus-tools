QA Block 10:
CLARIFIED QUESTION: What are the FDA’s guidelines for including low positives and fresh samples in retrospective studies for rapid antigen tests, including requirements for frozen dry swabs?
CLARIFIED ANSWER: The FDA recommends including 20% low positives in retrospective studies for rapid antigen tests, but frozen dry swabs should not replace fresh samples where the device intends fresh sample use. Five fresh positives are required, and flexibility is allowed for post-market completion of fresh sample studies. Freezing swabs could improve assay performance by making more target available but isn’t practical for point-of-care sites.
VERBATIM QUESTION: I have a question about retrospective studies that are done for a rapid antigen test. The template says that there should be 20% low positives. So yes we are recommending a minimum of five positives of fresh samples as the device is intended to be used. And we are providing a huge amount of flexibility in allowing the completion of the fresh sample studies post market if everything up to that point where we can make authorization decisions looks good.
