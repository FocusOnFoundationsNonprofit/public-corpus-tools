CLARIFIED QUESTION: What are the recent additions to FDA authorizations for COVID-19 tests?
CLARIFIED ANSWER: Recent additions to our authorizations include more pooling authorizations to identify asymptomatic positive patients, more home collection kits (including the first for both SARS-CoV-2 and flu detection), another rapid antigen test, and more serology tests.

CLARIFIED QUESTION: What are the FDA's current highest priorities regarding COVID-19 test development and review?
CLARIFIED ANSWER: The FDA's current highest priorities include point-of-care tests, home collection kits and home testing opportunities, panel tests, and extremely high throughput systems for efficient testing of large sample volumes. These priorities have not changed recently.

CLARIFIED QUESTION: Can the FDA provide guidance on establishing a primer set for LAMP assays, and if a general letter of reference from the CDC would allow us to avoid repeating certain validations?
CLARIFIED ANSWER: Yes, the FDA can provide guidance on this matter. A general letter of reference, similar to what the CDC did for PCR primers, could indeed help in reducing the need for repeated validation. The specifics of inclusivity, in silico analysis, and cross-reactivity would still need to be addressed in coordination with FDA guidelines.
VERBATIM QUESTION: I have a question about establishing a primer set for LAMP. They - I’ve - I’m with a public benefit corporation called FloodLAMP and we are developing a colorimetric LAMP-based assay that has a ultracheap front end and then goes into the colorimetric LAMP. And we're coordinating with other test developers who also want to open source our test to provide very low cost scalable options for essentially exactly the scenario that you described above in terms of the screening of - repeated screening of asymptomatic population. And I just want to understand that dynamic a bit more in terms of the inclusivity in silico analysis and the in silico cross-reactivity and the wet cross-reactivity. Can you sort of speak to that and give us some sort of guidance as we coordinate on this?
