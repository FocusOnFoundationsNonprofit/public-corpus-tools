QUESTION: What guidance does the FDA provide for establishing a primer set for LAMP testing, particularly regarding validation and cross-reactivity analysis?

QA Block 4:
QUESTION: What are the requirements and pathways for establishing a shared primer set in LAMP-based assays for COVID-19?
ANSWER: Establishing a shared primer set for LAMP-based assays can follow two main pathways. First, developers may obtain individual authorization for a test kit and then provide a right of reference for others to replicate the test. Despite this, kit developers must still seek individual authorization, and labs replicating the method are not covered under the original EUA. Alternatively, a method may be authorized under a model similar to Yale's saliva direct, which allows the originating entity to designate labs under their EUA scope based on established commitments to the FDA. In either case, using identical suppliers and catalog numbers for primers and probes simplifies the process, and open-source methods may enhance flexibility and adoption.
