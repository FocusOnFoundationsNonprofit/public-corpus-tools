CLARIFIED QUESTION: Can you provide guidance on establishing a primer set for LAMP, specifically regarding inclusivity in silico analysis, in silico cross-reactivity, and wet cross-reactivity?
CLARIFIED ANSWER: When it comes to LAMP-based assays and sharing a primer set, it's crucial to focus on standardizing evaluation methods. You can indeed reduce duplication by establishing common primers and validating them together. For inclusivity, the primers should be evaluated using an in silico analysis on a comprehensive database to ensure they match all known SARS-CoV-2 sequences. For in silico cross-reactivity, confirm that these primers do not match non-target organisms by running them against other coronaviruses and common respiratory pathogens. Wet cross-reactivity must also be tested in a laboratory setting to confirm these in silico findings. Documentation of these results and shared validation data can support multiple developers using the same primers without redundant testing.
