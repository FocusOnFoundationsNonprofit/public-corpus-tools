VERBATIM QUESTION: Can you clarify what the current highest priorities are for the FDA regarding COVID-19 tests?
VERBATIM ANSWER: Yes, our current highest priorities are point of care tests, home collection kits and home testing opportunities, panels, and extremely high throughput systems that allow for the most efficient testing of large volumes of samples. These priorities are key both in terms of review and in encouraging development from the development community.
CLARIFIED QUESTION: What are the current highest priorities for the FDA regarding COVID-19 tests?
CLARIFIED ANSWER: The FDA's current highest priorities for COVID-19 tests are point of care tests, home collection kits and home testing opportunities, panels, and extremely high throughput systems enabling efficient testing of large sample volumes. These priorities guide both the review process and the encouragement of development within the community.
