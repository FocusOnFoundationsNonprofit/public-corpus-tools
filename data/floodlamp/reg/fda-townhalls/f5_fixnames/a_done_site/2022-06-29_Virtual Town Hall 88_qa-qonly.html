3. Throat Swabs for COVID-19 Testing: FDA's Current Stance

3-1. Has there been any further research to validate throat swabs for at-home COVID-19 diagnostic tests?
Research has been conducted on throat swabs, but most evidence does not support their use, and the FDA has not authorized it. Throat swabs should only be collected by trained healthcare providers, and nasal swabs remain the recommended option for most COVID-19 tests. The FDA is open to throat swabs if adequate safety and performance data are provided.
