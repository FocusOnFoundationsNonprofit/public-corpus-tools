2. FDA Guidance on External and Internal Controls

2-1. What are the recommendations for external controls?
FDA recommends external positive controls for point of care and CLIA labs to be about five times the LOD of the test for verification. Controls must be validated in analytical and clinical studies. At-home molecular tests generally require internal controls but not external ones. Third-party external controls labeled for IVD use do not need to adhere to the EUA holder quality system.

VERBATIM QUESTION: What are the recommendations for external controls?

16. Enrichment Strategies for OTC Antigen Test Clinical Studies

16-1. Is the enrichment strategy still an option for clinical study designs for OTC antigen tests?
The FDA is open to enrichment strategies for OTC antigen tests as long as they do not introduce bias favoring the candidate test, and the collected data accurately reflects the test's performance. Sponsors should provide study design and justification to the review team or through a pre-EUA process.
