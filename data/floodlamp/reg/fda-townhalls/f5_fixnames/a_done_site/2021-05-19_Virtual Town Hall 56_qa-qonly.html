4. Analytical Study Requirements for 510k SARS-CoV-2 Test Submissions

4-1. What analytical studies are required to support a 510k submission for molecular SARS-CoV-2 tests?
FDA requires analytical studies to use naturally occurring clinical matrix unless unavailable. For selective studies excluding specimen stability and LOD, synthetic matrices may be used if their performance parallels that of natural specimens. Simulated matrices must mimic clinical specimens and undergo equivalence tests. A pre-submission is recommended to confirm validation approaches.
