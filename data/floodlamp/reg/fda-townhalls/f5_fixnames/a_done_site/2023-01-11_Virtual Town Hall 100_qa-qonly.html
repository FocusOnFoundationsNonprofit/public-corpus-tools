2. Guidance on SARS-CoV-2 Test Development Controls

2-2. Can SARS-CoV-2 test developers use plasma and serum depleted of anti-SARS-CoV-2 antibodies as negative controls for serology and neutralizing antibody tests that have already been granted approval?
The FDA recommends using pre-December 2019 samples as controls. Antibody depletion could alter sample composition, necessitating validation to avoid false results. 3. Comparator Requirements for Multi-Analyte Respiratory Panel Approval

3-1. Could a molecular influenza COVID multiplex emergency use assay be used as the comparator assay to support a multi-analyte flu and COVID antigen respiratory panel for de novo or 510k?
The FDA recommends using a highly sensitive FDA-cleared RT-PCR test cleared within the past five years for flu as a comparator. Older tests may be considered if their performance remains consistent with current flu strains, demonstrated via in silico analysis. For COVID-19, an EUA-authorized or FDA-cleared highly sensitive RT-PCR test with chemical lysis and solid-phase extraction should be used, validated with clinical samples, showing CT values and at least 95% PPA clinical performance. A pre-submission is advised to discuss studies for de novo or 510k.
