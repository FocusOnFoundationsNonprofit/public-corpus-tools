1-1. What steps are recommended to validate a test with the transport media identified in its intended use when changing the volume of collection medium?
FDA recommends validating your test with the transport media from its intended use, specifying tested volume during LOD testing. For tests with extraction steps, a clinical evaluation with larger volume samples can assess the worst-case scenario.
