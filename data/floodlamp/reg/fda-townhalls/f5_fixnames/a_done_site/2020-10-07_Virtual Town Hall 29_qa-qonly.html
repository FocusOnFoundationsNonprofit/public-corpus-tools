12-8. Are there different validation requirements for combo tests that include analytes previously reviewed and authorized by the FDA versus new analytes?
There are different validation requirements depending on whether the analytes in the combo test have been previously authorized by the FDA. Tests adding SARS to already cleared analytes like Flu A/B require less work than those involving new analytes which demand greater validation, including point-of-care studies.
