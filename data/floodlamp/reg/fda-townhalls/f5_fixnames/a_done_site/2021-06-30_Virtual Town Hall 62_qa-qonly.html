1. Developing and Validating SARS-CoV-2 Diagnostic Tests: Updates and Guidance

1-1. What type of study is required to demonstrate matrix equivalency for a proprietary assay buffer compared to viral transport media (VTM)?
The FDA recommends conducting a matrix equivalency study by testing assay performance in both VTM and the proprietary assay buffer at the same target levels across simulated and natural matrices. If equivalency is shown between sample types, an LOD analysis is acceptable, and repeating other validation studies is generally not needed.
