2. Guidelines for Monkeypox Test Development and Validation

2-1. Can an EUA-authorized monkeypox test be used for discordant analysis between the candidate device and the CDC test?
Yes, an EUA-authorized monkeypox test can be used for discordant analysis if it is a PCR test validated for similar specimen type, has high sensitivity, and includes chemical lysis with nucleic acid extraction, as specified in FDA templates. Results from discrepant analysis should not affect NPA and PPA calculations but can be footnoted.
